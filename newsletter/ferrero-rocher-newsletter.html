Not a birthday. Not a public holiday.
 

Just a Tuesday.

 

Nobody circles a Tuesday. Nobody plans a Wednesday. Yet that’s where life actually happens: the quick hello, the favour returned, the dinner that runs a little long.

 

So skip the reason and keep the gesture. Bring something small. Make it gold.

A note. A little gold. No reason needed.

Calendars mark the special days. Gold marks the ordinary ones.
Three ordinary moments
 

One little gesture
each time.
